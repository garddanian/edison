Engineering methodology

From site data to investment-ready decisions.

Our project process is designed to align technical feasibility, operational priorities and commercial objectives.

01 / Discovery

Site and demand assessment

Load profiles, electricity tariffs, operational priorities, available area and grid connection constraints.

02 / Modelling

System simulation

Evaluate PV yield, storage dispatch, peak shaving, charging loads and alternative system sizes.

03 / Architecture

Technology integration

Define power conversion, EMS, protection, controls, thermal and expansion provisions.

04 / Business case

Phased implementation planning

Compare technical scenarios, expected value drivers, risks and future expansion options.
