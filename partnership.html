Three ways to work with EDISON-G.

01 · Advisory

Engineering & technical advisory

Independent feasibility, sizing, equipment evaluation, electrical concept and procurement advice. You retain supplier and installer selection and their contractual relationships.

02 · Integration

System integration & project engineering

We develop the technical interfaces, coordinate requirements and support commissioning with customer-appointed suppliers and installers, within an agreed scope of responsibility.

03 · Turnkey

Complete turnkey delivery

Where agreed, EDISON-G serves as a single contractual counterpart and assumes defined overall responsibility for integrating the contracted equipment, works and subcontractors.

Your freedom of choice

Prefer your own technology partners?

That is compatible with our engineering-led approach. You can nominate manufacturers, components or installers; we assess their technical suitability, required interfaces and the responsibilities involved before agreeing the delivery structure.

For turnkey projects, customer-nominated equipment and partners require appropriate technical approval and contractual terms. If suppliers are contracted directly by the customer, responsibilities are allocated accordingly rather than presented as an unconditional single-point guarantee.

Turnkey accountability

One partner. One contract. One point of responsibility.

For an agreed turnkey scope, customers should not need to mediate between EDISON-G-appointed equipment suppliers and installers when integration or execution problems arise. EDISON-G manages those interfaces and remedies under its contract.

Final commitments, exclusions, performance criteria, warranties and liabilities are set out in the signed project contract.

Scalable by design

Build what you need today. Plan for tomorrow.

Capacity expansion is assessed from the start: equipment architecture, physical space, electrical interfaces, controls and grid connection provisions must support the intended development path.
